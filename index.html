How the numbers work

Yield is Receive Qty ÷ Make Qty, shown as a percentage rounded to 2 decimal places (0.97 = 97.00%). Totals use summed Receive ÷ summed Make for rows with positive Make and a known, non-negative Receive. Missing quantities are not zero and are excluded from weighted yield. Make and Receive totals each include all known values.

Only monthly production tabs are included. Pending reports and old daily tabs are excluded to avoid overlap. Row dates control the period, even when they differ from a tab's month. Values above 100.00% are kept and flagged; source data is never edited. DoD means day-wise and MoM means month-wise, not previous-period growth.
